TALK & HEAL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Talk & Heal is the private psychotherapy practice of Çiğdem Taş, a BACP-registered psychotherapist based in London, UK (talkandheal.co.uk). This page explains what data we collect and how we use it — both through our website's booking system and through the social media channels we manage on Talk & Heal's behalf.

What we collect

Through our website, we collect the information you provide when booking a consultation or contacting us: name, email address, phone number (for WhatsApp reminders where opted in), and payment details (processed directly by Stripe — we never store card numbers).

Through social media platforms you choose to follow or message us on (Instagram, Facebook, Threads, YouTube, X, Bluesky, Mastodon, Pinterest, TikTok, LinkedIn, Google Business), we may access basic public profile information and messages you send us, solely to respond to enquiries and publish our own content.

How we use it

Data is used only to: (1) schedule and manage your therapy appointments, (2) send booking confirmations and reminders by email/WhatsApp, (3) process session payments and refunds via Stripe, and (4) respond to enquiries sent via our social media channels. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Data retention

Booking and session records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and meet professional record-keeping obligations. Social media messages are kept only as long as the platform itself retains them.

Data deletion requests

You can ask us to delete any personal data we hold about you at any time by emailing tascigdem1977@gmail.com. Requests are handled within a reasonable timeframe.

Terms of use

By using this website or contacting Talk & Heal through any of our social media channels, you agree that: (1) information shared with us is accurate to the best of your knowledge; (2) the terms of service of any third-party platform you use to reach us (Meta, Google, X, etc.) also apply; (3) booking a consultation does not itself constitute the start of a therapeutic relationship until confirmed by Çiğdem Taş. Questions can be sent to tascigdem1977@gmail.com.

Contact

For any question about this policy: tascigdem1977@gmail.com
